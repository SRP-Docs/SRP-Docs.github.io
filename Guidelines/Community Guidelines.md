Below you will find our Discord Community Guidelines. Please be sure to read them carefully to avoid punishments, if you have any questions please open a ticket via our Discord server.

1. Respect. Whilst in SRP you must remain respectful at all times. Treat others how you want to be treated.
2. Swearing. Swearing is not permitted at SRP. Swearing will result in a ban.
3. Spam. Spamming of any kind will result in a timeout. This includes links, images, text.
4. NSFW Material. Sending any NSFW material will result in an instant ban.
5. Threats. Threatening anyone for any reason will result in punishment.
6. Racial Slurs. Using any racial slurs will result in an instant ban.
7. Discord Guidelines. You must follow all Discord Guidelines at all time. https://discord.com/guidelines

We reserve the right to moderate you at anytime we deem it necessary, while in Springfield Roleplay you accept these rules and accept the fact you may be moderated at anytime.
