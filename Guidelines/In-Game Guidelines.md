Below you will find our in-game guidelines. Please be sure to read them carefully to avoid punishments, if you have any questions please open a ticket.

  1. RDM. Random death match, this is killing someone without being in RP.
  2. VDM. Vehicle death match, this is someone damaging your vehicle while not in RP.
  3. NLR. New life rule, once you die in-game you must forget all info in your last RP, you cannot return to scene after death.
  4. FRP. Fail roleplay, when a user fails to roleplay or breaks the current roleplay. 
  5. LTAP. Leave to avoid punishment, leaving the game to avoid being moderated by staff.
  6. Cuff Rush & Auto Jail. The act of cuffing a user without saying -cuffs- before cuffing. After cuffing you must transport the user to the prison then jail them.
  7. Disrespect. You must respect all other players and staff.
  8. Safe Zones. Shootings/killing in safe zones will result in punishment. Safe zones include: All spawn points.
  9. Unrealistic Avatars. Your avatar must be realistic, unrealistic avatars include: Headless, large accessories, animal RP.
  10. Roblox ToS. You must follow Roblox's terms of service at all times. [!ref target="blank" text="Roblox ToS"](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us/articles/203313410-Roblox-Community-Standards)

We reserve the right to moderate you at anytime we deem it necessary, while in the in-game Springfield Roleplay server you accept these rules and accept the fact you may be moderated at anytime.
