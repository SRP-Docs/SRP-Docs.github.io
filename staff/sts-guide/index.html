Springfield Roleplay

STS Guide

Notes:

  • There must be at least 2 other staff members online;
  • You must have permission from the Directive Team+;
  • You must remain professional throughout the whole STS;
  • Set up the STS on Maple Street, the Sheriff Station or Highway 55;
  • If required use "!clear" before sending commands;
  • Wait at least 30-45 seconds between messages.

STS Messages

Copy and Paste into ER:LC

:m A STS will be held in 5 minutes, start wrapping up all roleplays.

:m A STS will be held, in 1 minute, please ensure all roleplays are complete.

:m STS Starting, bring all incoming.

:bring all (x2) OR :load others (x1) + :bring all (x1)

:m Hello and welcome to this STS at Springfield Roleplay. Please remain present and still until instructed to move.

:m Our Staff here at SRP have noticed a large amount of rule breaking within the server, therefore have decided to host this STS. If you go AFK at any time you will be kicked.

:m We will now start with the server rules.

:m RDM | Random Death Match is killing someone without being in RP. Doing so will result in a warning.

:m VDM | Vehicle Death Match is shooting or damaging another players vehicle while not in RP. Doing so will result in a warning.

:m Staff RDM | Staff Random Death Match is killing an on-duty staff member. Doing so will result in a kick.

:m Staff VDM | Staff Vehicle Death Match is shooting or damaging a staff vehicle. Doing so will result in a kick.

:m NLR | New Life Rule is once you die you must start a new life and cannot return to your old life. Doing so will result in a warning.

:m LTAP | Leave to Avoid Punishment is leaving while a staff member is giving you a punishment. Doing so will result in a ban.

:m FRP | Fail Roleplay is failing to roleplay or breaking the current roleplay you are in. Doing this will result in a warning.

:m Cuff Rush | When arresting a user you must say "-cuffs-" before placing them in cuffs. Failure to do so will result in a warning.

:m Auto Jail | After arresting a user you must transport them to the jail before jailing them. Failure to do so will result in a warning.

:m Exotics | You must be a VIP or server booster to use exotic vehicles. Without being VIP or a server booster you will be asked to changed the vehicle.

:m Disrespect | You must remain respectful while playing SRP. Member disrespect will result in a warning, staff disrespect will result in a kick.

:m Safezones | All safezones include all spawn points. Shooting or killing in these areas will result in a warning.

:m Unrealistic Avatars | You avatar must be realist while please SRP. You will be asked to change your avatar if it includes headless, large accessories or animal RP (Without permission.).

:m Staff Impersonation | Impersonating a staff member will result in a kick.

:m Mod Call Abuse | Exploiting the !mod call system will result in a warning and/or kick.

:m We will now conduct an avatar check. Please remain on the STS line until told to move. If you are told to change you avatar please follow the instruction.

:m The avatar check is now complete.

:m The STS is now complete. Once loaded you can return to Springfield and continue your roleplays. Enjoy!

:load all

Once all players have been loaded, please head to Melonly Moderator Panel > Actions > STS Log and complete the log, once complete resume normal staff operations.
